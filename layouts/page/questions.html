How This Works 如何使用

Each question is a hub page. Tag your notes, readings, and policy memos with the question they address. The question hub then automatically collects:

  • Reading Notes — what you read to investigate
  • Research Notes — analysis and hypotheses
  • Policy Notes — policy implications
  • Conclusions — where your thinking landed
