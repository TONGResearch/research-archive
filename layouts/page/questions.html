Research Questions

研究问题

This is the complete question database of the archive. Questions remain here across their full lifecycle: featured, long-term, open, partially answered, and closed.

For me, research begins with the discipline of asking better questions. My idea of Slow AI is not a rejection of artificial intelligence, but a way of using it more carefully: think first, clarify the problem, form an initial judgment, then use AI as assistance and finally verify its output.

In this sense, the archive follows a simple principle: do not rush to obtain answers. Learn first to formulate better questions. / 不要急于获得答案，先学会提出更好的问题。
