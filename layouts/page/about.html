Biography 简介

I am a university student working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, governance, and political economy. My research draws on political science, sociology, and economics to examine how technology reshapes institutions, labour, and social organization.

This archive documents my intellectual development from the start of my sophomore year. It is intended as a long-term record of reading, research, and evolving understanding — a public account of how my thinking changes over time.

Research Agenda 研究路线

My current research focuses on how AI systems reshape production relations, governance capacity, and regulatory frameworks — particularly in comparative perspective between the EU and China.

Research Questions / 研究问题

  • How does AI reshape production relations?
  • How do algorithmic systems influence governance capacity?
  • What forms of regulation are appropriate for platform economies?
  • How should states respond to emerging AI systems?

{{/* ── Research Philosophy ── */}}

Research Philosophy 研究理念

I believe that rigorous long-form thinking requires public accountability. Every note here is a work in progress. The name 未竟 — "unfinished" — is deliberate. Research is never complete.

I favour interdisciplinary approaches, primary sources over summaries, and policy documents as empirical material. I try to hold hypotheses lightly and revise them publicly.
