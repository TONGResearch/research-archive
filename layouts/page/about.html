Biography 简介

I am a university student working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, governance, political economy, and public policy.

My current interests focus on how emerging technologies reshape institutions, education, labour, and public decision-making. I approach AI not only as a technical system, but also as a social and political force that changes how societies evaluate knowledge, organize production, design policies, and define human capability.

This website is a long-term research archive rather than a personal blog. It records my reading, research questions, essays, working hypotheses, and intellectual development. I use it to document how my interests gradually move from broad questions about AI and society toward more focused research on technology governance, institutional design, comparative policy, and political economy.

The archive is intended for academic development, future research collaboration, and long-term self-reflection.

Research Agenda 研究路线

My current research focuses on how AI systems reshape public decision-making, educational evaluation, institutional capacity, production relations, and human development.

Research Questions / 研究问题

  • Can AI genuinely improve public decision-making, or does it merely make decisions appear more scientific?
  • Is the central problem of technology governance a lack of scientific knowledge, or a lack of effective institutions?
  • How much authority should experts have in technology governance?
  • What should education evaluate in the age of AI?
  • Can human value transcend capital logic?
  • How is artificial intelligence transforming the relations of production?

Research Philosophy 研究理念

I believe that rigorous long-form thinking requires public accountability. Every note here is a work in progress. The name 未竟 — "unfinished" — is deliberate. Research is never complete.

I favour interdisciplinary approaches, primary sources over summaries, and policy documents as empirical material. I try to hold hypotheses lightly and revise them publicly.
