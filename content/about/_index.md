I am a university student interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence, governance, political economy, sociology, and public policy.

This website serves as an open research archive where I document my reading, research progress, intellectual development, and evolving understanding of contemporary social and technological change.

Rather than presenting finished conclusions, this archive records ongoing questions, working hypotheses, research notes, and policy-oriented reflections.

Current interests include:

- AI and social transformation
- Digital governance
- Platform economies
- Public policy design
- Marxist political economy
- Computational approaches to social research

This archive is intended for long-term learning, academic development, and future research collaboration.
