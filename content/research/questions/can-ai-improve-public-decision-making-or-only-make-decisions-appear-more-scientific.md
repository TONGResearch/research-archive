question_description: >-
  Artificial intelligence is increasingly used in public administration, policy analysis,
  risk assessment, and decision support. It may improve the speed, consistency, and evidence
  base of public decision-making. However, AI may also make political or value-based decisions
  appear more objective and scientific than they really are. This question examines whether AI
  genuinely improves public decision-making or merely changes its appearance.

scope: >-
  This research focuses on AI-assisted governance, public administration, decision support systems,
  algorithmic accountability, state capacity, and the political limits of technical decision-making.

objectives: >-
  To investigate whether AI can improve public decision-making while preserving accountability,
  legitimacy, transparency, and human judgment.

current_position: >-
  My current view is that AI can improve some aspects of public decision-making, especially in
  information processing, pattern recognition, and administrative coordination. However, AI cannot
  eliminate political judgment. The risk is that technical systems may make contested decisions
  appear neutral, objective, or inevitable.
