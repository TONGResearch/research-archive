question_description: >-
  Technology governance is often framed as a problem of insufficient scientific knowledge.
  However, many governance failures may arise not because knowledge is absent, but because
  institutions cannot interpret, coordinate, legitimate, or act upon available knowledge.
  This question investigates whether the core problem of technology governance lies in
  knowledge deficits or institutional deficits.

scope: >-
  This research focuses on institutional design, regulatory capacity, public administration,
  expertise, uncertainty, and the relationship between knowledge and governance.

objectives: >-
  To examine whether better scientific knowledge alone can improve technology governance,
  or whether effective institutions are necessary to transform knowledge into legitimate
  and actionable policy.

current_position: >-
  My current view is that scientific knowledge is necessary but not sufficient for effective
  technology governance. The deeper challenge often lies in building institutions capable of
  interpreting uncertainty, coordinating actors, responding to risks, and maintaining legitimacy.
