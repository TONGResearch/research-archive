question_description: >-
  Technology assessment aims to evaluate the possible social, economic, ethical, and political
  consequences of emerging technologies before they become fully embedded in society. However,
  emerging technologies are surrounded by uncertainty, unintended consequences, and changing
  social contexts. This question asks whether technology assessment can genuinely anticipate
  long-term consequences.

scope: >-
  This research focuses on technology assessment, foresight, risk governance, uncertainty,
  scenario analysis, and the limits of prediction in emerging technology governance.

objectives: >-
  To examine whether technology assessment can provide reliable guidance for long-term
  governance, or whether its main value lies in making uncertainty visible and improving
  institutional preparedness.

current_position: >-
  My current view is that technology assessment cannot fully predict the future, but it can
  improve governance by clarifying possible pathways, exposing assumptions, identifying risks,
  and helping institutions prepare for uncertainty.
