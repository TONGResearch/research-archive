question_description: |-
  Artificial intelligence is often portrayed as a force that may replace or diminish human capabilities.
  But could the opposite also be true?
  As AI increasingly performs repetitive, computational, and procedural tasks, might human beings become freer to cultivate creativity, judgment, responsibility, empathy, and moral agency?
  This research explores whether AI can ultimately contribute to a deeper realization of human potential.
scope: |-
  This research examines artificial intelligence, education, labor, creativity, human agency, technology ethics, and future society.
  It focuses on how AI reshapes the meaning of being human, rather than automation alone.
objectives: |-
  To examine AI's impact on human agency;
  To explore how AI reshapes education and labor;
  To identify the uniquely human capacities that remain essential in the age of AI;
  To investigate whether AI can contribute to human flourishing.
current_position: |-
  I currently believe that the greatest challenge posed by AI is not competition between humans and machines.
  Instead, it is whether humans remain willing to cultivate the capacities that define humanity itself.
  AI may not diminish humanity; rather, it may reveal more clearly what it truly means to be human.
