question_description: |-
  Every society faces the challenge of balancing competing interests.
  How can governments reconcile the interests of minorities and majorities, capital and labor, efficiency and equality, while maintaining social stability and legitimacy?
  This research explores institutional approaches to balancing competing interests in modern governance.
scope: |-
  This research focuses on public policy, democratic governance, distributive justice, social contracts, wealth distribution, and institutional mechanisms for reconciling competing interests.
  It does not seek to eliminate conflicts, but to understand how they may be governed.
objectives: |-
  To examine the origins of social conflicts;
  To analyze institutional approaches for balancing competing interests;
  To evaluate the role of public policy in promoting social cohesion;
  To explore governance models that combine efficiency, fairness, and long-term stability.
current_position: |-
  I currently believe that conflicts of interest cannot be permanently eliminated.
  However, they can be continuously managed through institutional design, public participation, and effective governance.
  The long-term legitimacy of any political system depends on its ability to balance competing interests fairly.
