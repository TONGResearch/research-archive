question_description: >-
  Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming how people learn,
  work, create knowledge, and interact with society.
  
  In response, education appears to face a fundamental choice:
  
  Should educational institutions adapt to technological change,
  redesigning curricula and assessment systems to prepare students
  for an AI-enabled world?
  
  Or should education actively shape the direction of technological
  development by promoting values, judgment, responsibility,
  and human flourishing?
  
  This research explores the relationship between education and
  technological change in the age of AI.

scope: >-
  This research focuses on higher education,
  AI-related educational policy,
  curriculum design,
  assessment systems,
  and technology governance.
  
  It examines how educational institutions respond to technological
  change and how they may influence the future development of AI.
  
  Technical implementation details are outside the scope of this research.

objectives: >-
  To investigate the relationship between education and technological change;
  
  To evaluate the strengths and limitations of adaptation-oriented and
  shaping-oriented approaches to education;
  
  To examine whether the fundamental mission of education changes in the age of AI;
  
  And to explore how education can balance innovation,
  human development,
  and the public good.

current_position: >-
  I currently believe that education should neither reject AI nor
  merely adapt to it.
  
  If education only follows technological change,
  it risks losing its ability to shape the future of society.
  
  If it ignores technological transformation,
  it risks becoming increasingly disconnected from reality.
  
  Therefore, I favor a dual approach:
  education should adapt to AI while simultaneously shaping its future direction.
  
  Educational institutions should help students develop AI-related capabilities,
  while continuing to cultivate judgment,
  responsibility,
  critical thinking,
  and civic values.
